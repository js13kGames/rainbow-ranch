Congratulations!  You are the proud owner of serene Rainbow Ranch and 9 stunning Unicorns.

It is February 14, 2027 and here is the Ranch Report.

The pristine herd of unicorns at Rainbow Ranch are under threat.  Unfortunately, thanks to youtube's Fireship idea, rogue AI agents have made Horse Dating App a reality.  It has thrown the equine industry into mayhem.

Donkeys are the most prolific users of the app and they are causing you great stress.  To guard the unicorns from unsuitable suitors, you and your dog must patrol the field to scare the donkeys away before they elope.

For desktop; WASD for movement , Q & E for looking around.

For VR, requires a browser with **hand-tracking enabled**.  Hand gestures; hand point, pinch and thumbs up to move and look around.

Made with THREE.js
